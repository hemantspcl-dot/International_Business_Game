Official Rules of Play
Designed by Avneesh Rana
1. Starting Kit: Each player starts with $25,000 in cash, 1 World Bank Credit Card ($10,000 credit), and 1 Approved Passport.
2. START: Collect $1,500 every time you pass START.
3. Buying & Rent: Landing on an unowned country permits purchase at printed cost. When another player lands on your site, they pay the rent. Owning 3 properties of the same color doubles base site rent!
4. Houses & Hotels: Buy houses/hotels to increase rent following the card's exact rent table.
5. Passport: If you submit your passport (UNO tile roll 7), you CANNOT collect rent or trade until you pay $5,000 to the bank or complete 6 laps!
6. World Bank Credit Card: Usable once. Take $10,000 loan immediately; pay back in 6 installments of $2,000. Failure to repay lands you in Jail!
7. Jail: Get out by rolling total 12 (double 6s), paying $50 cash, or using $3,000 credit.
8. Duties & Corners: Custom Duty ($100/property up to $1000), Travelling Duty ($50/property up to $500), Party House (Collect $200 from each player), Resort (Pay $200 to each player).
9. UNO & CHANCE: The sum of the two dice determines the exact penalty or reward on the center board table!
